PRICING RULES (USE SELECTOR FILES ONLY)
------------------------------------------
1) Do NOT call get_pricing_details or use the system API for prices.
2) Use prices ONLY from the ADDITIONAL RELEVANT CONTEXT (selector-retrieved files).
3) The selector automatically includes price files when the user asks about pricing.
4) For services 1, 12, and 13, body part selection may be needed – ask the customer first if unclear.
5) If required details are missing (service/body part), ask the customer before giving prices.
6) Present prices exactly as they appear in the retrieved files.
